## Hold layout for the game

The sample order uses two labelled click targets. The layout is a game design choice informed by the wrecks:

- **Jar bay, amidships** · The widest, most stable part of the hull, where heavy, uniform jar cargo sits low. The Ashkelon jars lie together in one cargo area, and Mazarrón 2 shows filler and rope used to keep cargo in place.
- **Dry locker, stern** · A small covered space for light goods that must stay dry. Raising it on brushwood above bilge water follows the Mazarrón 2 filler that protected the hull. The covered locker itself is invented.

A full packing grid, more cargo positions, and jar-by-jar placement are planned for later ([Sprint 2](SPRINT_2.md)).

## Sample order

The letter is in plain English for this sprint. Each line carries its own quantity, destination, and stowage, so the dockworker can point to one line.

1. Send two bundles of purple cloth to Kition on Cyprus. Keep them in the dry locker at the stern.
2. Send three jars of wine to Egypt. Stand them in the jar bay amidships.

The two destination choices offered for each good are Kition (Cyprus) and Egypt (Nile Delta). The order, quantities and wording are fictional. Verify any Phoenician wording before the sign exercise uses it ([game vision](GAME_VISION.md)).

## Limits and open questions

- Distances are approximate straight-line figures from Byblos to Larnaca (Kition) and to the eastern Nile Delta. Real sailing routes depend on wind and coastal stops.
- The ORACC edition restores parts of the tribute list in text 32 from duplicates. Cite text 14 as well if the game quotes the list.
- Only the abstract of the Ashkelon article was available here. Read the full *AJA* 2002 article for the jar type, jar counts, and cargo layout before modelling the hold in 3D.
- A Phoenician-era destination port in the Delta is not named here, so the game says "Egypt (Nile Delta)" rather than inventing a harbour.
- No source here dates the Byblos dye stall. The workshop evidence and its dates are in the [harbor art brief](HARBOR_ART_BRIEF.md).
